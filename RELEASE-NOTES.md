## GoTrack! 0.5.0 – 43 languages

**New:** every European language plus Japanese and Simplified Chinese (43 in total). The language follows your system language automatically and can be changed in Settings.

**Downloads**
- **Windows:** `GoTrack-Setup-0.5.0.exe`
- **Android:** `GoTrack-0.5.0.apk` (allow installing from this source; updates install over the previous version)
- **iPhone / Mac / browser:** open the web app (GitHub Pages of this repository) and use "Add to Home Screen" / "Install".

**Note:** Translations are AI-generated and not yet reviewed by native speakers – corrections are welcome via Issues.

**⚠️ Please export your data regularly (Settings → Export), especially in the browser/iPhone version.**
